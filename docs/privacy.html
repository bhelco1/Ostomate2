Ostomate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your data stays on your device

Ostomate stores all your supply and change event data locally on this device using a private database. Your data is never sent to the developer, and never to any analytics or advertising service. Only two optional features, both off by default, can send anything off your device: crash reporting, and (on Android) a daily backup to your own Google Drive — see below.

No analytics or tracking

Ostomate contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no telemetry of any kind. We do not know how you use the app.

No account required

Ostomate works without creating an account or providing any personal information. Your name, email, and identity are never collected. If you turn on the optional Google Drive backup, you sign in through Google's own screen; Ostomate never sees your password and does not ask Google for your name or email.

Backups

The Export feature creates a backup file on your device that you can save wherever you choose. If you have turned on the optional daily Google Drive backup (below), the Backup button instead saves that file straight to the same "Ostomate backups" folder in your own Google Drive.

Daily Google Drive backup (optional, Android)

The Android app has an optional daily backup to Google Drive. It is off by default and is not shown in the app's regular settings. If you turn it on, Google asks you to allow Ostomate access to only the files Ostomate itself creates in your Drive (Google's "drive.file" permission); Ostomate cannot see or change any of your other files.

Once a day, Ostomate then uploads the same backup file the Export feature makes — your supplies, your change history and your app settings — to a folder named "Ostomate backups" in your own Google Drive, over an encrypted connection directly from your device to Google. Backups you make with the Backup button while this is on go to the same folder. It keeps the 14 newest backups and deletes its own older ones. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else; storage in your Drive is covered by Google's privacy policy.

You can turn the daily backup off at any time. Backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Crash reporting (optional)

Ostomate includes optional crash reporting powered by Sentry, which is disabled by default. If you choose to enable it in Settings → Security, only crash stack traces are sent — no personal data, no usage events, and your supply or event notes are never included. You can disable it at any time.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Ostomate removes all app data from your device. If you used the optional Google Drive backup, its files remain in your own Google Drive; delete the "Ostomate backups" folder there to remove them. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
